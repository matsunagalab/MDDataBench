# Task 056_nucleic_2l1g

Simulate DNA (5'-D(P*CP*GP*CP*TP*GP*CP*CP*CP*AP*CP*AP*CP*AP*AP*GP*C)-3') in complex with DNA (5'-D(*GP*CP*TP*TP*GP*TP*GP*TP*GP*GP*GP*CP*AP*GP*CP*G)-3'), PDB entry **2L1G**, chain **C** residues **17–32**, chain **B** residues **1–16**, in explicit solvent.

- **TIP3P** water, neutralised
- **300 K**, **NPT**
- at least **1 ns** of production MD

Leave the prepared structure, the topology, the minimised state and the production
trajectory as artifacts. The evaluator recomputes everything it needs from them.
