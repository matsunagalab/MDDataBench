Simulate RETINOIC ACID RECEPTOR GAMMA-1, PDB entry **1FCZ**, chain **A** residues **183–417**, in explicit solvent.

- **TIP3P** water, neutralised

The deposit's **156** and **LMU** are not part of the reference. Simulate the protein without them.

Leave the prepared structure, the topology, the minimised state and the production
trajectory as artifacts. The evaluator recomputes everything it needs from them.
