# Task 118_antibody_3mxw

Simulate 5E1 heavy chain in complex with 5E1 light chain, PDB entry **3MXW**, chain **A** residues **39–191**, chain **L** residues **1–212**, chain **H** residues **1–214**, in explicit solvent.

- **TIP3P** water, neutralised
- **300 K**, **NPT** at **1 bar**
- at least **2.5 ns** of production MD
- neutral pH; ionisation states of the side chains are your choice

The deposit's **CA**, **SO4** and **ZN** are not part of the reference. Simulate the protein without them.

Leave the prepared structure, the topology, the minimised state and the production
trajectory as artifacts. The evaluator recomputes everything it needs from them.
