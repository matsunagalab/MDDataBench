# Task 119_antibody_5y9j

Simulate Tumor necrosis factor ligand superfamily member 13B in complex with belimumab heavy chain, PDB entry **5Y9J**, chain **L** residues **2–211**, chain **H** residues **2–224**, chain **A** residues **142–285**, in explicit solvent.

- **TIP3P** water, neutralised
- **300 K**, **NPT** at **1 bar**
- at least **2.5 ns** of production MD
- neutral pH; ionisation states of the side chains are your choice

Chain H does not resolve residues 138, 139, 140, 141, 142; the range runs through them, so build them.

Leave the prepared structure, the topology, the minimised state and the production
trajectory as artifacts. The evaluator recomputes everything it needs from them.
