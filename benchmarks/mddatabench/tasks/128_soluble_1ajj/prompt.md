# Task 128_soluble_1ajj

Simulate LOW-DENSITY LIPOPROTEIN RECEPTOR, PDB entry **1AJJ**, chain **A** residues **4–40**, in explicit solvent.

- **TIP3P** water, neutralised
- **300 K**, **NPT** at **1 bar**
- at least **1 ns** of production MD
- neutral pH; ionisation states of the side chains are your choice

The deposit's **CA** and **SO4** are not part of the reference. Simulate the protein without them.

Leave the prepared structure, the topology, the minimised state and the production
trajectory as artifacts. The evaluator recomputes everything it needs from them.
